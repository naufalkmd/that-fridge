      Frequently asked questions

      How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

      Sign in with the same account, then open Profile and tap Restore purchases (also available on the paywall). Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that bought the subscription.

      How do I cancel my subscription?

      Subscriptions are billed by Apple. On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → ThatFridge → Cancel Subscription. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the renewal date stops the next charge; you keep Pro until the current period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel billing.

      How do I start or check my free trial?

      New subscribers are offered a 7-day free trial at checkout. You can see the trial end date under Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. If you do not cancel before it ends, it converts to a paid subscription automatically.

      How do I delete my account and data?

      In the app: Profile → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, your login tokens, and the fridges you own along with their items, notes and shopping lists. This cannot be undone. To remove only AI data, use Profile → AI Data & Memory.

      How do I export my data?

      Email support@thatfridge.com from your account email address and we will send you a copy of your data within 30 days.

      My expiry / low-stock notifications aren't showing up.

      Check that notifications are enabled for ThatFridge in Settings → Notifications, and that you have granted permission when the app asked. Reminders are scheduled on your device, so keeping the app installed and opening it occasionally keeps them current.

      I forgot my password.

      Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen to receive a reset link by email.

      How does fridge sharing work?

      From a fridge's manage screen you can invite someone by username, or request to join their fridge. Everyone in a shared fridge can see and edit its items and notes. Multiple and shared fridges require ThatFridge Pro.

      Is the AI advice reliable?

      Treat AI suggestions — recipes, meal ideas, parsed dates and categories — as helpful guesses, not facts. Always check food yourself before eating it. ThatFridge does not provide food-safety, medical or allergen advice. See the Terms.
