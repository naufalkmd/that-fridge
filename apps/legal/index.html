Questions

Before you download.

Is ThatFridge free?

Yes. The free plan covers fridge and pantry tracking, expiry alerts and 50 AI credits a month. Pro adds 400 credits a month that roll over, more than one fridge and a shared household fridge, for US$2.99 a month or US$19.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

What are AI credits?

Every AI action spends credits: crew chat, receipt and fridge-photo scans, and icon generation. Free accounts get 50 a month, Pro gets 400, and you can top up with credit packs anytime.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. It's in the works. Join the Android waitlist and we'll email you once when it's out.

What happens to my fridge photos and receipts?

When you scan a receipt or a fridge photo, the image is uploaded to our server and sent to an AI vision provider to read the items. Images stay on our server so the feature keeps working, and you can delete the items anytime. Read the privacy policy.

Can my household share a fridge?

Yes, with Pro. Invite someone by username from a fridge's manage screen, and everyone in it sees and edits the same items and notes.

How do I cancel the free trial?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. On your iPhone go to Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, ThatFridge, Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
