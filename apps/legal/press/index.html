      About ThatFridge

      One line

      ThatFridge is an iPhone app with a crew of four pixel-art AI helpers that tracks your fridge, tells you what to use first, and helps you waste less food.

      Short

      ThatFridge is a shared household food app for iPhone. Add a whole shop at once by scanning a receipt, a fridge photo or a barcode, or type it in. A crew of four AI helpers takes it from there: Guardian watches expiry dates, Chef suggests meals from what you already have, Organizer keeps items in the right place, and Shopkeeper tracks what's running low.

      Long

      A lot of food gets thrown away simply because nobody remembered it was there. ThatFridge keeps track so you don't have to. Scan a receipt or a photo of your fridge, scan a barcode, or type items in, and every item gets a food group, storage spot, shelf life and pixel icon.

      A crew of four pixel-art AI agents does the remembering. Guardian sends expiry reminders and tells you what to use first. Chef turns what's already on your shelves into meal ideas, starting with what's about to expire. Organizer files every item in the right place, and Shopkeeper builds the shopping list from what's running low. You can chat with any of them.

      A household can share one fridge, so everyone sees the same shelves, notes and shopping list. Meal plans, recipes and a weekly Kitchen Score round it out. ThatFridge is free to download, with ThatFridge Pro for more AI credits, more fridges and household hosting.
